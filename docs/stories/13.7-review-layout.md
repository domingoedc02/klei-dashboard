# Story 13.7: Upload Review: layout

As a Staff member,
I want the Review screen to lay out like the prototype with nothing overlapping,
So that I can read every group and field and the save bar never covers them.

**Assumptions**
- Prototype: `js/upload.js` lines 28-67, `css/screens.css` lines 56-78 (`.rv` grid, `.rv-list` max-height 600 and inner scroll, `.gl` bordered card, `.gl[aria-current=true]` accent border + 1 px ring, `.savebar` sticky bottom, border-top, padding space-4/space-8), `.banner`. Desktop: `desktop/src/upload/Review.tsx` (BAND line 26, h2 228, group `button.row` 234, `.footer` 264, Missing-a-tag hint 327), `styles.css` lines 226-258.
- Pairs: `upload-review`, `upload-review-low-group`, `upload-review-scrolled`, `upload-review-discard-dialog`, plus the new `upload-review-long` (13.1).
- This story is layout only: grid, scroller, cards, savebar, banner, subtitle, copy. The selectable photo grid, inline Move/Merge/Split and the removal of the Actions menu are 13.8. Until 13.8 lands the Actions menu is moved inside the editor card header (not floating) so 13.7 can ship alone as the hotfix.
- **Decision D5 (UR-16), pending the user's answer.** Assumed option (a), the recommendation: keep `Undo` (it protects staged edits, story 11.6) and place it left of `Discard batch` in the savebar. The rest of the bar matches the prototype: hint left, `Discard batch` + primary `Confirm and save N gowns` right.
- UR-11: the confidence bar leaves the list card; the list shows the pill only. The detail header gets the inline `Confidence 72%` + 64 px bar in 13.8 with the editor card; here the bar is simply removed from the list.
- UR-17 and the editor card structure (UR-05): right-hand bordered card (radius 12, padding 24) containing Name, Photos, Organize, tags grid, description. Photos and Organize bodies are 13.8; this story creates the card and the 3-column tags grid (`repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))`), and drops the "Missing a tag?" hint (not in the prototype).
- UR-21 is closed with the `upload-review-long` fixture: the name truncates with an ellipsis and the pill keeps its size.
- Reuse from superseded 12.5/12.6: footer bleed and z-index fix, scroll clearance, list `max-height: 600px; overflow-y: auto; padding: 2px`, the card list content (name + pill line, up to five 40 px thumbs, `N photos · Silhouette · Fabric`).

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** the Review screen (UR-01, UR-22)
**When** it renders and is scrolled
**Then** the savebar is full-bleed and sticky at the bottom of the content pane with a top border, padding space-4/space-8, hint on the left and buttons on the right; no text or control is under it and no pair of text elements overlaps

**Given** the group list (UR-03, UR-06, UR-07, UR-10, UR-11, UR-21)
**When** 10 groups render
**Then** the heading is `Gown groups`; each card is bordered with name (ellipsis) and pill on one line, up to five 40 px thumbs and `N photos · Silhouette · Fabric` (missing parts omitted); the current card has the accent border and 1 px ring; the list scrolls inside itself above 600 px; no confidence bar is in the card

**Given** the page frame (UR-04, UR-08, UR-09)
**When** the page renders at 1440x900
**Then** the content column reaches the same right edge as the prototype (x=1408), the subtitle "Drop photos. AI groups the same gown, tags it, and drafts a description." shows under `AI Upload`, and the banner is the neutral grey `.banner` with the spark icon and bold lead "30 photos → 10 gowns."

**Then** it is a bordered card to the right of the 320 px list with the label "Gown name (auto-named, editable)", a 3-column tags grid, and no "Missing a tag?" hint
