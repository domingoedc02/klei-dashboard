# Story 12.7: Upload and Processing pass

As a Staff member adding photos,
I want the drop zone, the upload progress and the grouping screen to look and fill the page like the wireframe,
So that the first screens of the upload flow do not look cramped or unfinished at the 1100px minimum window.

**Assumptions**
- Audit rows G-10 (page frame: `.upload` was capped at 880px, uncapped in 12.2), G-19 (proc grid, fixed to `minmax(0, 1fr)` in 12.5), plus the layout drift below. Story 11.5 fixed the copy, the steps and the Cancel dialog and ended "Not verified: manual side-by-side"; this story verifies and aligns the geometry only. **No copy, behaviour or request changes.**
- Prototype: `js/upload.js:24` (steps), `:31-38` (processing), `:93-96` (empty); CSS `css/screens.css:43-56`: `.drop` (1.5px dashed `#8a8580`, `--radius-lg`, `min-height: 440px`, centred, padding `--space-8`, hover/over: accent border and tint, `:focus-within` ring), `.drop-inner` (column, gap `--space-3`, `max-width: 440px`), `.proc` (card, `max-width: 640px`, `margin: var(--space-8) auto 0`, gap `--space-6`), `.step .mk` (20px circle, 1.5px border; done = filled with check; current = accent border with a pulsing 8px dot), the processing grid (`--od-cols: 10; --od-gap: 6px`, 30 cells, `aspect-ratio: 3/4`, cells already done are flat `#d9d6d3` with no shimmer).
- App today (`desktop/src/styles.css:188-210`, `:215-224`, `:468-474`; `Upload.tsx:183-251`; `Processing.tsx:46-98`): `.dropzone` has a 2px dashed border, no minimum height, padding 32px, so it is about 220px high (prototype 440); `.processing` has no card, centring or max-width and runs full content width; the steps use text glyphs `✓ ● ○` in a 1.25em column; the grid is six tiles in three columns (11.5 chose six "group tiles"); the bar is a bare `<progress>` below the steps.
- Decisions (veto-able in review):
  - **Dropzone**: `min-height: 440px` at 1280x800 and `min-height: 320px` at 1100x700 via one `@media (max-height: 760px)` rule (the window is 700 high at the minimum and 440 plus head plus queue would push the batch strip off screen; this is a height query, not a width one, so the 12.2 rule against sub-1100 width queries does not apply). Dashed border 1.5px `--color-control-edge`; background `--color-frame`; `.over` keeps `--color-accent-tint`; `:focus-within` shows the focus ring; inner column `max-width: 440px`, centred.
  - **Processing**: wrap in the 12.3 `.card`, `max-width: 640px`, `margin: var(--space-8) auto 0`. Header (h2 + percent) as built; the overall `<progress>` moves directly under the header (prototype `:33`) with the 8px bar style; the steps list follows; the Cancel button is secondary and left-aligned below the grid.
  - **Step markers**: a 20px circle (`.mark`): done = filled `--color-ink-2` with a check icon (`Icon name="check"`), current = accent border with a pulsing dot (no animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`), todo = empty ring. The text glyphs go; the `sr-only` " (done)" and `aria-current="step"` stay.
  - **Skeleton grid**: prototype's 30 photo cells in 10 columns, gap 6px, `aspect-ratio: 3/4`, `aria-hidden`; cells below `floor(pct * 0.3)` render flat grey without shimmer. This replaces 11.5's six tiles: the cells now stand for photos, which is what the screen is about (the app's cap is 200, the grid stays 30 as a decoration; the count is not tied to the batch size). `[ASSUMPTION]` accepted as a purely visual change; `Grouping.test.tsx:135` asserts 6 children and changes to 30.
  - **Hints** "You can leave this screen…" and "Photo thumbnails are sent to your AI provider." stay (11.5/10.x privacy requirement) as `.hint` lines under the card content, not removed because the prototype lacks them.
  - **Queue** (`running`): the existing status line + `<progress>` + Cancel get `.card`; no copy change.
  - **Rejected list**: a bordered block with the `bad` tone (prototype `.banner.bad`: tint `--color-pill-neg-bg`, ink `--color-pill-neg-ink`) instead of a loose paragraph; text and actions unchanged; check contrast with axe (DESIGN.md pill ink pairs are AA).
  - **In this batch** (stored photos): app-only feature, kept; thumbnails become a `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(96px, 1fr))` grid with the 96px squares, `gap: var(--space-2)`; the Remove "×" keeps its 24px target.
  - **Page head**: the prototype has a subtitle under the h1 ("Drop photos. AI groups the same gown, tags it, and drafts a description.", `js/upload.js:28`). Add it as `p.hint.sub` under the h1 **only in the empty phase**; `[ASSUMPTION]` the sentence is already on the wireframe-inventory list for Upload; if `docs/ux-spec.md` or DESIGN.md says no page subtitles (check "page head" in DESIGN.md), drop it and record that in the Dev record.
  - **Wireframe-state toggles** (`Empty / Processing / Review / Error`, `stsel`) and "Try with 30 sample photos" are wireframe tools and are **not built**.
- Not changed: `MAX_PHOTOS` stays 200 (U-1; removed 2026-11-14 by Story 21.6), the Cancel dialog, `overallPct`/`stepLabel`, upload and processing logic, the Review screen (12.5, 12.6).

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** the Upload screen with no batch at 1280x800
**When** the harness renders `upload-empty` (prototype `css/screens.css:43-47`)
**Then** the drop zone spans the content width (no 880px cap), has a 1.5px dashed border, is at least 440px high, and the icon, h2, hint and **Choose photos** are centred in a column no wider than 440px. At 1100x700 it is at least 320px high and the stored-batch strip stays reachable without the page scrolling more than one screen. Test: `styles.test.ts` asserts `.dropzone` has `min-height: 440px`, the `@media (max-height: 760px)` rule has `min-height: 320px`, and `.dropzone:focus-within` sets a focus ring.

**Given** the drop zone
**When** the pointer drags a file over it or focus moves inside it
**Then** the drag-over class `over` still applies the accent tint (existing test `Upload.test.tsx:200`), and keyboard focus on **Choose photos** shows both the button ring and the zone ring. axe: zero violations.

**Given** a batch is being uploaded
**When** the queue renders
**Then** it shows in a `.card` with "Uploading k of n", the progress bar at full width and Cancel; no text changes. Existing `Upload.test.tsx` queue tests pass unchanged.

**Given** some files were rejected
**When** the summary renders
**Then** it is a tinted bordered block with `role="alert"`, the count sentence, the list with the reason per file, **Retry upload** and **Dismiss**; ink on tint passes axe `color-contrast` (run once with the rule enabled in this test). Existing rejection tests pass.

**Given** a batch is processing (step 2 of 4, 15 of 30 done)
**When** `Processing` renders at 1280x800 (prototype `css/screens.css:48-56`, `js/upload.js:31-38`)
**Then** the content is one card, at most 640px wide and horizontally centred, with the h2 "AI is grouping images" and "37%" on one row, the progress bar directly under it, the four steps with 20px markers (step 1 a filled circle with a check, step 2 an accent ring with a dot, steps 3-4 empty rings), a 30-cell, 10-column grid of 3:4 cells of which the first `floor(37 * 0.3) = 11` are flat grey, then the two hint lines and **Cancel**. Test: `Grouping.test.tsx` asserts the `.proc-grid` has 30 children, 11 of them with the `done` class, `aria-hidden="true"`, the marker states by class (`li.done .mark svg`, `li.current .mark`, `li.todo .mark`), `aria-current="step"` on the current step, and the progressbar value 37.

**Given** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`
**When** Processing renders
**Then** no dot pulse and no shimmer (`styles.test.ts` asserts the reduced-motion block lists `.mark` dot and `.skeleton`).

**Given** the Processing screen at 1100x700
**When** the harness renders `upload-processing`
**Then** all checks pass; the grid has ten equal columns (`minmax(0, 1fr)`), no horizontal overflow, and Cancel is visible without scrolling.

**Given** the Stopped (error) state
**When** it renders
**Then** it is a bordered block in the same card width with the alert text, cause and **Retry**; copy unchanged. Existing `Grouping.test.tsx` Stopped tests pass.

**Given** the page head in the empty phase
**When** the heading renders
**Then** the subtitle sits under the h1 (if kept after the DESIGN.md check) and is absent in processing and review.
