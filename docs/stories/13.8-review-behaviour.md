# Story 13.8: Upload Review: select, Move, Merge, Split

As a Staff member reviewing a batch,
I want to tick photos in a group and move, merge or split them from a panel on the page,
So that I do not hunt for them in "Actions" and "…" menus, and the screen behaves like the design.

**Assumptions**
- Reuses the design of superseded story 12.6 (read `docs/stories/12.6-review-inline-move-merge-split.md` for the full detail: staged `Op` list model, enable rules, copy, removed code, test mapping). Decisions inside that story still stand unless the user vetoes them: native `select` instead of the prototype combobox; the "New group" option on Move is dropped (Split creates the new gown); `Discard selected` stays as a fourth button; the photo lightbox stays via a corner `Open photo k of M` button; Undo stays.
- Prototype: `js/upload.js` lines 55-60 and 97-132 (acts `up-cur`, `up-sel`, `up-move`, `up-split`, `up-merge`), `css/screens.css` `.thumbs`, `.th`, `.tick`, `.org`, `.org-row`, `.combo`. Pairs: `upload-review`, `upload-review-photos-selected`, `upload-review-combo-open`.
- Selection is local UI state in `Editor` (`string[]` of photo ids), not in `History`; derive `picked` from the current photos so Undo or a group switch cannot leave a stale id; clear after any op.
- Staged-edit model, commit request, leave guard and version from story 11.6 are unchanged. No API change.
- The accepted deviation (native `select` vs prototype combobox) is recorded in the audit row UR-15 as `accepted deviation` with this reason. The capture states `photos-selected` and `combo-open` must now show the state their names promise (they were `pending: 13.8` in 13.1): update `shots/desk/upload.mjs` `after` hooks.
- Enable rules (from 12.6): Move needs at least one photo and a target; Merge needs a target; Split needs at least one and fewer than all photos selected, else help "Leave at least one photo in this gown."; Discard selected needs at least one.

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** a group with N photos (UR-13)
**When** the editor renders
**Then** the heading is "Photos in this group (N)" with the muted hint "Select photos to move or split" at its right, and the photos are a six-column `minmax(0, 1fr)` grid of toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`, 20 px tick box, accent border when pressed); a live line reads "2 photos selected."

**Given** two photos are selected (UR-14)
**When** the panel renders
**Then** `Move 2` is enabled once a target is chosen and the capture `upload-review-photos-selected` shows two ticked photos and the enabled button

**Given** the Organize panel (UR-02, UR-15)
**When** it renders
**Then** three rows in prototype order: "Move selected photos to" + Move, "Merge this whole gown into" + Merge, "Split selected photos into a new gown" + Split, each with its help line; the `Group actions` and per-photo `…` menus no longer exist and `Menu`/`Picker` are gone from `Review.tsx`

**Given** a target is chosen and Move, Merge or Split is pressed
**When** the action runs
**Then** photos move, the group merges or a new gown "{name} II" is created with the source's tags and description; toasts read `Moved 1 photo to Group 2`, `Merged into Group 2`, `Split into a new gown. Check its tags.`; no request is sent; one Undo step restores the state

**Given** no photo selected
**When** the panel renders
**Then** Move, Split and Discard selected are disabled with the 12.6 help lines

**Given** the editor header (UR-02)
**When** it renders
**Then** Name and the inline `Confidence 72%` + 64 px bar + pill share one row, and nothing floats beside the heading

**Given** `axe.run` on Review in four states (none selected, two selected, merge target chosen, empty batch)
**When** it runs
**Then** zero violations; the Organize group has an accessible name

**Given** the story's screens are captured on both sides
**When** `node scripts/capture.mjs --side both --only {only}` and `npm run shots:diff -- --only {only}` run
**Then** `geometry.json` shows no `overlap`, `covered`, `glued-text`, `text-clipped`, `offscreen-x`, `page-h-scroll`, `dialog-scroll` or `dialog-overflow` for the desktop side of these screens (`text-ellipsis` only where the prototype truncates the same text), and the run logs no `UNMOCKED` request and no page or console error
