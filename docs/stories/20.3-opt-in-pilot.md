# Story 20.3: Opt-in pilot

As the owner, I want a few volunteers to use the Electron preview next to the Tauri app for a fixed period, so that real use finds what scripted checks miss.

**Assumptions**
- No users exist yet (project fact); "volunteers" are the owner and any people the owner invites. The pilot is optional and the owner may waive it in the decision record.
- Pilot builds are the unsigned preview artifacts from 19.4 from the download site (18.5). Gatekeeper and SmartScreen steps from 18.2 are given to each volunteer.
- The preview uses its own app id, profile and credential entry, so volunteers keep the Tauri app installed.
- No telemetry is added. Feedback is by a written form and by the owner's own notes (NFR-6).

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** the pilot start
**When** volunteers are given the install guide
**Then** it is the Electron-specific page (`electron/INSTALL-PREVIEW.md`, not the protected `docs/install-guide.md`) with: how to install, the OS warning steps, how to switch back, how to report a problem, and what data is shared with nobody.

**Given** the pilot period (default two weeks, set by the owner)
**When** it ends
**Then** `docs/planning-artifacts/electron-migration/evidence/pilot.md` lists each volunteer's OS, version, days used, problems found (with owning story), and whether any lost or duplicated data was reported (the stop condition).

**Given** a problem of severity "lost or duplicated business data", "cannot sign in" or "app does not start"
**When** reported
**Then** the pilot stops, the issue is filed against its owning story, and 20.4's cutover record cannot be marked PASS until the story is fixed and the affected rows of 20.1 are re-run.

**Tasks**
- `electron/INSTALL-PREVIEW.md`.
- `docs/planning-artifacts/electron-migration/evidence/pilot.md` template and results.

**Dev notes**
- Reference only: `docs/install-guide.md` (protected; do not edit), `docs/release-checklist.md`.
- Pilot feedback never includes business content; screenshots must hide client data.

**Testing**
- Human activity; no code. Not run here.

**Prototype unchanged check**
- Require the 14.1 gate. Volunteers' Tauri installs are checked to start and sign in after the pilot.
