# Story 11.4: Dashboard fidelity

As a Staff member,
I want the Dashboard to greet me, show four key figures and three clear widgets,
So that I see what needs me today without opening three screens.

**Assumptions**
- Prototype: `js/dashboard.js`. Current: `desktop/src/dashboard/Dashboard.tsx` (h1 "Dashboard", four widgets), `backend/app/src/main/kotlin/co/klei/app/dashboard/DashboardService.kt` (`view` at line 52), `DashboardIT.kt`, `Dashboard.test.tsx`.
- First task is to confirm the rows marked "verify": the exact stat sub-lines and the Recent uploads row layout in `dashboard.js`, and where `InquiryService` keeps the first message time of an Inquiry.
- User decision: match the prototype **except** the date line suffix. The line is "Saturday, Nov 7" only; "· 3 appointments today" is not built (no appointments feature exists).
- The h1 becomes the greeting. This reverses `DESIGN.md` line 308 ("Never a greeting") and the matching `ux-spec.md` line; both are corrected in this story. One h1 per screen stays true.
- `[ASSUMPTION]` Greeting follows the hour in the User's Preferences timezone: 05:00 to 11:59 "Good morning", 12:00 to 16:59 "Good afternoon", otherwise "Good evening". The prototype hard-codes "Good morning". First name is the first word of `me.name`.
- Date line uses the Preferences timezone, format "Saturday, Nov 7" (weekday long, month short, day), English only (no localisation in scope).
- Layout order: page head (greeting, date line, primary **Upload photos** button, shown only with `PERM_UPLOAD_IMAGES`), four stat cards, then widgets Recent uploads, AI processing, Unread inquiries.
- Stat cards (label / figure / sub-line), FR-7 scoping kept: a card is absent when the caller's Role may not see its data.

  | Card | Needs | Figure | Sub-line | Empty sub-line |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Total gowns | `PERM_VIEW_GALLERY` | all Gowns | "+{n} this week" | "Nothing saved yet" |
  | Unread inquiries | `PERM_REPLY` | unread New count | "Oldest from {time}" | "Inbox is clear" |
  | Photos this month | `PERM_VIEW_GALLERY` | Photos of Gowns saved this calendar month | "Across {n} gowns" | "Upload to get started" |
  | Avg. reply time | `PERM_REPLY` | mean first-reply time, last 30 days | "Down from {x}" or "Up from {x}" | "No replies yet" (figure "—") |

- `[ASSUMPTION]` Definitions, all computed in the service, none stored:
  - "Photos this month": Photos whose Gown has a `dayBidx` in the UTC days from the 1st of the current month to today (the existing `DayBucket` mechanism, at most 31 values). Month edges follow UTC, not the Preferences timezone. `// shortcut: UTC month edges, switch to per-User buckets if the studio reports off-by-a-day at month end.`
  - "Oldest from": the lowest-`seq` Inquiry that is New and unread; time is its `lastMessageAt`, shown as a time ("8:15 AM") if it is today in the Preferences timezone, otherwise a date ("Nov 5").
  - "Avg. reply time": over the latest 100 Inquiries by `seq` (message dates are encrypted, so no SQL range). For each Inquiry take its first `IN` message time and its first `OUT` message time after it; unanswered Inquiries are skipped. Current window: first `IN` within the last 30 days. Previous window: 31 to 60 days. Figure is the mean of the current window; the sub-line compares it with the previous mean and is omitted when the previous window has no replies or the two are equal. Format: under 60 minutes "{m}m", under 48 hours "{h.h}h", else "{d.d}d".
  - `// shortcut: 100 newest Inquiries only, add a cached daily figure if a studio gets more than 100 Inquiries per 60 days.` The Assistant never sends (FR-27), so every `OUT` message is a staff reply.
- Widgets:
  - Recent uploads: up to 5 rows, each with a 40 px thumb, the Gown name, "{silhouette} · {n} photos" (just "{n} photos" when no silhouette), and a blue **New** pill when `isNew`.
  - AI processing: header pill gray **Idle**, blue **Checking…** (a batch is processing), green **Ready for review**, warn **Stopped** (`[ASSUMPTION]`, not in the prototype; the batch is in `error`). Processing shows a bar and "{step name} · {done} of {total} photos"; Review shows "Grouped into {n} gowns · {k} need a quick look." and a **Review groups** button to `#/upload` (with `PERM_UPLOAD_IMAGES`); Error keeps today's line.
  - `[ASSUMPTION]` For Review, `aiOf` returns `groups` and `lowConfidence` (groups under 80). This reads the batch's groups, which `aiOf` avoided so far ("no group is decrypted here"); it is one batch, already read when Review opens. Only the Review phase pays this.
  - Unread inquiries: up to 3 rows as today. Empty: "No new inquiries", "New website inquiries will appear here.", **Open inbox**.
  - Recent uploads empty: "No uploads yet", "Upload gown photos and AI will group them for you.", **Upload photos** (with `PERM_UPLOAD_IMAGES`).
- The 30 s poll and the inquiry-event refresh stay. While loading, four stat skeletons and three widget skeletons show; no layout jump.
- API: `DashboardView` gains `photosThisMonth`, `gownsThisMonth`, `avgReplySeconds`, `prevAvgReplySeconds` (all nullable); `InquiryWidget` gains `oldestUnreadAt`; `AiStatusView` gains `groups`, `lowConfidence`. `docs/api/openapi.json` and `desktop/src/api/schema.d.ts` are regenerated (`npm run types`). Month and reply figures sit under the existing `total` and `inquiries` error names, so a failure shows the widget error block, not a new key.

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** an Admin at 09:10 in the Preferences timezone
**When** `Dashboard.test.tsx` renders with fake time
**Then** the h1 reads "Good morning, Ada", the date line reads the weekday, month and day with no appointments text, and **Upload photos** is a primary button; at 14:00 the h1 reads "Good afternoon, Ada" and at 20:00 "Good evening, Ada".

**Given** a Viewer (gallery only)
**When** the Dashboard renders
**Then** only Total gowns and Photos this month show among the stats, no Upload photos button shows, and no Inquiry text appears anywhere.

**Given** a Role with Reply only
**When** the Dashboard renders
**Then** only Unread inquiries and Avg. reply time show, and `DashboardIT` confirms the response holds no Gown field and no month figure.

**Given** a workspace with no Gowns, Inquiries or batch
**When** the Dashboard renders
**Then** the stats read "Nothing saved yet", "Inbox is clear", "Upload to get started" and "No replies yet" (figure "—"), the AI pill reads Idle, and both empty widget states show the copy above.

**Given** 96 Gowns of which 10 were added in the last 7 days and 42 Photos on 6 Gowns saved this month
**When** `DashboardIT` calls `GET /api/v1/dashboard`
**Then** `totalGowns` is 96, `newThisWeek` is 10, `photosThisMonth` is 42 and `gownsThisMonth` is 6; a Gown saved on the last day of the previous month (UTC) is not counted.

**Given** an unread New Inquiry created yesterday and one created today
**When** the Dashboard renders
**Then** the card reads "Oldest from" with yesterday's date (not a time) and the figure counts both.

**Given** `ReplyTimes.average` (pure function)
**When** `ReplyTimesTest` feeds rows
**Then** unanswered Inquiries are skipped, a reply before the first message is ignored, rows older than 60 days are ignored, the means for both windows are right, and an empty window returns null.

**Given** a current mean of 2.4 hours and a previous mean of 3.1 hours
**When** the card renders
**Then** the figure is "2.4h" and the sub-line "Down from 3.1h"; with a previous mean of 1.5 hours it reads "Up from 1.5h"; with no previous mean there is no sub-line.

**Given** a batch in `processing`, then `review` with 10 groups of which 3 are under 80, then `error`
**When** the AI card renders each state
**Then** the pill reads Checking… with a bar, then Ready for review with "Grouped into 10 gowns · 3 need a quick look." and **Review groups**, then Stopped with the existing sentence.

**Given** `RECENT` Gowns, one flagged `isNew`
**When** the card renders
**Then** each row shows thumb, name and "{silhouette} · {n} photos", and only the new one carries the **New** pill.

**Given** axe on the Dashboard in all states above
**When** `Dashboard.test.tsx` runs
**Then** zero violations (one h1, bars named, pills not colour-only).

**Given** the leak suite
**When** extended
**Then** the new figures are aggregates only, and a failing reply-time query logs the exception class, never row text.
