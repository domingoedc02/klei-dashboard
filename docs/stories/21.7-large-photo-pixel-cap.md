# Story 21.7: Large photo pixel cap

As a Staff User,
I want full-size camera JPEGs to upload,
So that I do not have to shrink 89 photos from the shoot before adding them.

**Assumptions**
- Reverses the 40 megapixel header cap of Story 4.1. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-14 after 89 files (`GP8A*.jpeg`) were refused as "over 40 megapixels".
- Not unlimited. JPEG and PNG now stop at 100 megapixels; WebP stays at 40. The cap is the decompression-bomb guard: a small file that claims a huge canvas must not reach a decoder.
- Why 100 is safe for JPEG and PNG: `ThumbnailService` decodes them at 1/n scale (`setSourceSubsampling`), so heap holds about 4 x (w/step) x (h/step) bytes, near 4 MB for any size, not 4 x w x h. WebP has no scaled decode and is read whole at 4 bytes a pixel (40 MP = 160 MB), so it keeps 40. At most two decodes run at once.
- The 25 MB file limit is unchanged. A 100 MP JPEG over 25 MB is still refused as too large.

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** a JPEG or PNG whose header is 99 megapixels and whose file is 25 MB or less
**When** the User adds it
**Then** it is stored and a 480 px thumbnail is made

**Given** a JPEG or PNG header over 100 megapixels, or a WebP header over 40
**When** it is added
**Then** the server answers 413 `too_large` without `extensions.limit`, and the app lists the file as "Image is too large: over 100 megapixels (WebP: 40)" and carries on with the rest
