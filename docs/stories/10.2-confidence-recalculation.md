# Story 10.2: Confidence recalculation

As a Staff member,
I want the Confidence pill to drop when a group holds a photo that does not match the rest,
So that the groups that need my eyes lead the review instead of showing High.

**Assumptions**
- Today stage A is the mean pairwise cosine ×100 (`Clusterer.confidence`), and stage B is `0.7*sim + 0.3*share*100` (`ConfidenceScorer.score`, `DescribeRules.kt`). `share` is the taxonomy hit rate, not a same-gown measure, and it can lift the score above the similarity (full share lowers the High bar from similarity 90 to 86).
- New stage A: `round(100 * (0.5*mean + 0.5*minPair))`, where `minPair` is the lowest pairwise cosine in the group. A group of two has one pair, so the result equals that pair. A singleton stays 0.
- New stage B: `sim * (0.7 + 0.3*share)`, truncating as before. Share can only lower the score. Share 0 gives `0.7*sim`; share 1 gives `sim`.
- `[ASSUMPTION]` Band cut-offs stay 90 and 80 here (`confidenceBand`, PRD glossary line 83). Story 10.4 re-fits them from eval data and edits the glossary and the `bands` test together.
- Stored confidence of existing groups is not recomputed. No migration. Batches in `review` keep their numbers; only new runs use the new formula.
- Rearrange (`RearrangeService`) keeps its fixed 70 for new groups and does not recompute on merge or move. Out of scope.

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** a group of four photos with three near-identical vectors and one outlier
**When** `ClustererTest` computes its confidence
**Then** the result is lower than the mean-only value by the half-weight on the lowest pair, and lower than the same group without the outlier.

**Given** a group of two photos
**When** confidence is computed
**Then** it equals the rounded similarity of that single pair, as before.

**Given** a singleton
**When** confidence is computed
**Then** it is 0 and the band is `low`.

**Given** `ConfidenceScorer.score(similarity, mapped, named)` with `mapped == named` (share 1.0)
**When** `DescribeRulesTest` runs
**Then** the result equals `similarity` for every value 0 to 100, so the taxonomy can never lift a group.

**Given** `named = 0` or `mapped = 0` (share 0)
**When** score is computed
**Then** the result is `trunc(0.7 * similarity)`.

**Given** the two reported gowns (ivory satin off-shoulder, wrap bodice vs pleated bodice, same mannequin)
**When** their eval vectors from Story 10.1 are added to a fixture and clustered at the default sensitivity
**Then** if they still merge, the group's confidence is below 90 (not High). `[ASSUMPTION]` this fixture is added only if the operator supplies the vectors; otherwise a synthetic pair with cosine 0.78 and a third photo at 0.95 to both stands in.

**Given** the bands
**When** `ClustererTest.bands` runs
**Then** it still pins 90 High, 80 Medium, below 80 Low (unchanged in this story).

**Given** the leak suite
**When** extended
**Then** no new stored field exists, so no change; the existing `GroupingIT` leak assertions still pass.
