# Story 11.10: Settings structure, tab keyboard and copy match the prototype

As a Staff member or Admin,
I want Settings arranged and worded like the approved prototype,
So that I find each setting where I was shown it.

**Assumptions**
- User decision (2026-11-10): adopt the prototype structure. Four personal tabs (Profile, Signature, Preferences, Notifications) and, for people who may see admin pages, one **Admin** tab with an "Admin only" badge.
- Admin sub-tabs: Staff, Roles & permissions, AI settings, Audit log (prototype) plus **AI usage** and **Keys**, which exist only in the desktop. `[ASSUMPTION]` they are added as the fifth and sixth sub-tabs, in the order Staff, Roles & permissions, AI settings, AI usage, Keys, Audit log.
- Default tab is Profile for everyone (today an Admin lands on Staff). The route keeps working: `#/settings/staff` and the like still open that sub-tab.
- Roving `tabindex` with ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/Home/End (the comment in `Settings.tsx:14` promised it).
- The 2FA card (Story 11.14), Away message (11.12) and the email columns (11.13) arrive in their own stories; this story only leaves their places.
- First task: confirm the **verify** row for the AI tab against `prototype/v0.1.0/js/settings.js` (`adminAI`): sections "Model provider" and "AI settings", their separate save buttons and toasts ("AI settings saved").

**Acceptance Criteria**

**Given** Settings
**When** it opens
**Then** the h1 reads "Settings" with the line "Manage your account." (Admin: "Manage your account and workspace."), and the tabs, each with an icon, are Profile, Signature, Preferences, Notifications, and for permitted users **Admin** with an "Admin only" badge.

**Given** a Viewer or Staff member without admin permissions
**When** Settings opens
**Then** the Admin tab is absent and `#/settings/staff` shows Profile.

**Given** the Admin tab
**When** it opens
**Then** a button group labelled "Admin sections" shows the sub-tabs in the order above, each respecting its own permission, and the selected one has `aria-pressed="true"`.

**Given** focus is on a tab
**When** ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, Home or End is pressed
**Then** focus and selection move to the next, previous, first or last tab; only the selected tab has `tabindex="0"`.

**Given** Roles
**When** it renders
**Then** the heading reads "Roles and permissions"; the sub-tab label reads "Roles & permissions"; the toast "Permission updated" is unchanged.

**Given** Profile, Preferences and Staff copy
**When** they render
**Then** these strings match the prototype: Profile cards "Profile" and "Password" with the toasts "Profile saved" and "Password updated"; Preferences cards "Language and region" and "Inquiries" with the toast "Preference saved"; Staff heading "Staff ({n})", button **Invite staff**, dialogs "Invite staff" and "Edit staff member", the delete title "Delete {name}?" and body "{name} will lose access immediately. Their uploads and replies stay in the workspace. This can’t be undone.", toasts "Invite sent to {email}", "Invite re-sent to {email}" and "Changes saved", and the empty state "No staff yet" / "Invite teammates so they can upload gowns and reply to clients.".

**Given** the axe run
**When** it covers every tab and sub-tab
**Then** no serious or critical issues.
